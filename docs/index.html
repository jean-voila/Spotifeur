FAQ

Do I need a Spotify account?

No. Without one: YouTube Music and local files.

Do Free Spotify accounts work?

Yes, including playlists and offline playback. AAC 128 kbit/s on the tested Free account; up to 256 kbit/s with Premium.

Do I need the Spotify app installed?

No. The audio engine is inside Spotifeur.

Which phones are supported?

ARM64, Android 8.0 or later.

Is it synced with the official app?

Yes: saved tracks, albums, artists and playlists.
